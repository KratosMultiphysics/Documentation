Settings explanation DIMENSION TO COMPUTE: This symbolic generator is valid for both 2D and 3D cases.

Since the element has been programed with a dimension template in Kratos, it is advised to set the dim_to_compute flag as "Both". In this case the generated .cpp file will contain both 2D and 3D implementations. LINEARISATION SETTINGS: FullNR considers the convective velocity as "v-vmesh", hence v is taken into account in the derivation of the LHS and RHS. Picard (a.k.a. QuasiNR) considers the convective velocity as "a", thus it is considered as a constant in the derivation of the LHS and RHS.
